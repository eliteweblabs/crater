# Default Website Invoice Items

Here's a suggested list of common website-related services that could be included in an invoicing system:

## Website Development
- Custom website design and build
- Responsive design and mobile optimization
- WordPress/CMS development and integration
- E-commerce platform setup (Shopify, WooCommerce, etc.)
- Custom web application development

## Domain & Hosting
- Domain name registration and renewal
- Web hosting (shared, VPS, or dedicated)
- SSL certificate installation and renewal

## Branding & Graphics
- Logo design
- Brand identity guidelines
- Custom graphic design (banners, illustrations, etc.)
- Photography and image licensing

## Digital Marketing
- Search engine optimization (SEO)
- Google Ads/Facebook Ads campaign management
- Social media profile setup and management
- Email marketing platform integration

## Maintenance & Support
- Ongoing website updates and content management
- Plugin/theme updates and security patches
- Website monitoring and performance optimization
- Technical support and troubleshooting

This list can be customized based on your specific service offerings, but should give you a good starting point for common website-related line items to include in your invoicing process.
